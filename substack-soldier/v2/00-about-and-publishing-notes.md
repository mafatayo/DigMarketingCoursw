# About "Dispatches from the Line" (use on Substack's About page and subtitle)

**Subtitle:** A work of fiction. A 90-day series told in the voice of a Ukrainian soldier, about the war and the civilians it falls on.

**About text (suggested):**
*Dispatches from the Line* is fiction. The narrator, "Lys", his section, and the events in these pages are invented. They are written to reflect the war and its human cost as faithfully as fiction can: the shelters and corridors, the families split between a place and a phone, the old people on the stairs, the soldiers who were bank clerks and teachers.

It is not testimony, and nothing here should be read as a report from a real unit or a real battle. If you want first-hand accounts, look for verified testimony from Ukrainian soldiers and civilians, and from reputable humanitarian organizations such as the International Committee of the Red Cross and the UN Refugee Agency.

**Each post** ends with the line: *Dispatches from the Line is a work of fiction.*

**Do not:** present Lys as a real person in comments, replies, social posts, or any appeal for money. If you ever add a donation link, point it to a real, verifiable organization and say so plainly.

## v3 format (current)
`substack-soldier/v3/` holds the final structure for each Dispatch: bottom line, OPSEC & context, the reality on the ground, tactical observations, the human element, and (on Days 1, 30, 60, 90) a "not a fundraiser" help block pointing to established organizations. The evening Notes follow, each ending with the fiction line.

# v2 Story Bible (internal, not for publication)

Label on every post: *A work of fiction inspired by the accounts of Ukrainian soldiers.*

## Hard lines
- No real place names (beyond narrator's home city Kharkiv and the Chernihiv/Lviv/Poltava hometowns of cast), no real units, no real battles or incidents. General war texture only.
- No operational/tactical detail. Grim = human cost, fear, loss, damage at home. Not gore for its own sake.

## Voice rules
- Specific, odd, unglamorous details. Uneven sentence length. Humor survives even in the worst posts.
- No "I want to tell you", no moral wrap-ups, no closing questions to readers (except rare), few similes, no triads.
- Vary openings (mid-scene, dialogue, a flat fact) and endings (cut off, joke, silence, flat statement).
- Dispatch 550-800 words. Notes 80-200 words, rough field notes.

## Cast
- Narrator: Danylo "Lys", 34, sound engineer, Kharkiv. Wife Katya, daughter Sonya (6, sleeps in the corridor during alerts), brother Andriy, mother (hip, hospital Day 27). Rabbit toy Marmalade.
- Motor (Sgt, 53, bus mechanic, wife in Chernihiv, hums four bars of a bus-stop song). Vira "Magpie" (radio, 31, ex-bank clerk, Poltava). Bohdan (22->23, drones, apologizes to them, notebook of lost drones). Halyna (medic, 44, Lviv nurse). Zenko (41, history teacher, two scarves, daughter expecting). Pasha (bunk below narrator, "evacuated"). Yaroslav (19, replacement, arrives Day 18). Lt. Maksym (arrives Day 38). The Colonel (dog, arrives Day 13). Shtab (cat at command post). Lidiya (borshch driver). Taras (guitar, transfers out Day 54). Dmytro (Bohdan's friend, in hospital).

## Plot spine
- Pasha: Day 2 "evacuated, not killed". Day 14 word arrives he died of wounds at a stabilization point. Razor stays on the shelf.
- Act I (1-30): arrival, grind, first close call (Day 8), hope and small joys, bad news from home (Day 27), Zenko's daughter pregnant (Day 28).
- Act II (31-60): pressure builds. Drones, ammo rationing, power cuts, comms jamming, neighbors attacked. Day 35 evac night, casualty dies in van. Day 44-47 pressure/assault builds, Day 47 last calm day. Day 48 Bohdan is killed (drone team targeted). Day 49 chaplain. 50 notebook. 55 ledger of names. 59 narrator tells Dmytro in hospital. 60 reckoning.
- Act III (61-90): time jumps (note in Day 61). Winter, empty chair at holiday, video call cut by alert. Leave Days 65-68: Kharkiv, strike at night, corridor. Return. Spring, thaw reveals the dead. Lt. Maksym wounded (Day 78). Yaroslav learns to listen (mirror of Bohdan). Day 85 assault on the position: Zenko wounded, evacuated alive. Day 86 baby born news. Day 90: Yaroslav shaves with Pasha's razor; Motor hums the whole song; Bohdan's notebook gets a last line.

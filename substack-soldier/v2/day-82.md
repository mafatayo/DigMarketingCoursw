# Day 82 — Dispatch: The Bridge

**Bottom line:** Bohdan's bridge sketch is on our wall, and we agreed to build it after.

I found the bridge in the green notebook.

I wasn't looking for it. Vira had asked me to copy a list of numbers for the donors and I took the notebook down from the shelf for the first time since the evening she read it aloud. I turned past the columns, past the *Sorry*s, to the back cover. Inside it, in pencil, small and clean: a stone arch bridge. A graceful one, with a footpath on one side and a little lamp at each end. Under it, in student handwriting: *Village. Where the old one fell. After.* I sat with it.

I took it to the table. Zenko leaned over and took off his glasses. "It's very good," he said, in a voice that wasn't quite his. "An arch is the oldest hope. Every arch says, *we shall cross this.*"

Then Vira said, "We should build it."

"Vira—"

"I mean it." She had the look she gets at the radio. "Not now. After. Somebody should."

Within the hour, the rest of us had joined in. We pinned the bridge to the wall between the children's cards and the lists of names. Vira drew a school, a big bright building with proper windows and a courtyard with one very old tree. Halyna drew a clinic, low and white, with a veranda and a row of rocking chairs. Yaroslav drew a barn, with a cow. "Malvina's granddaughter," he said.

Motor didn't draw. But after a while he took the pencil and with enormous care, in his heavy workman's hand, drew a bus. A long kind-faced rounded bus with a destination sign and, in the window, a small face.

"What's the route?" I asked.

"To the end of the road. Where a woman waits with a thermos."

Nobody said anything. Zenko took off his glasses and polished them twice.

I drew a room with thick walls and a window onto a garden. A desk with a peg for headphones, a microphone on a stand, and a sign on the door: *QUIET. RECORDING.*

We pinned them up in a crooked gallery of the world that comes after. The Colonel sat in front of it with his head tilted, as if reading.

I should say that none of us said who'd begun it. We left the bridge in the center, at eye level, where you'd see it first. I noticed Vira reached up and straightened it, by a millimeter, before she went to bed.

# Day 82 — Notes: Who Builds the Bridge

**Bottom line:** Pay and name the people who build.

Bohdan drew a bridge. I'd like it built. And I'd like the people who build it to be paid properly and to be thanked and to have their names on a stone beside it.

It's the same thing I want for everyone.

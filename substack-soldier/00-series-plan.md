# "Dispatches from the Line" — 90-Day Substack Plan (180 posts)

**Format:** FICTION / composite narrator. Label every post: *"A work of fiction inspired by the accounts of Ukrainian soldiers."*

## Narrator (fictional)
- Callsign **"Lys"** (Fox), 34. Former sound engineer from Kharkiv. Mobilized, infantry support / drone team.
- Dry humor, observational, tender about small things, never graphic for shock value.

## Voice rules
- First person, present or close past tense. Short sentences. Concrete objects (tea, socks, power banks) over abstractions.
- No operational detail (no locations, unit names, positions, tactics that could be real). Keep it human, not tactical.
- No invented real-world events, casualty claims, or atrocity claims presented as fact. Emotion and daily life are the material.
- Morning = **Dispatch** (600–800 words, narrative). Evening = **Notes** (300–500 words, reflection/explainer/list).

## Cadence
- Dispatch 07:00, Notes 19:00 local. Paid tier: weekly voice-memo audio + Q&A (Days 89–90 mailbag).

## Arc
- **Days 1–30 — Before and Behind the Line:** routines, people, small survival.
- **Days 31–60 — The Work:** drones, cold, medics, fear, logistics, loss.
- **Days 61–90 — What Stays:** winter, leave, politics, memory, future.

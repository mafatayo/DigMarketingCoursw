# Day 72 — Dispatch: Anger at Corruption

I'll say it plainly, since I've been too polite: some of us are very angry about the people who steal.

It isn't a constant anger. Most days it sits low and quiet, like coals under ash. But now and then something blows on it. Last week it was a news story, a rumor, probably, though not necessarily false, about equipment gone missing, contracts that didn't make sense, men in good coats and clean boots getting rich while a boy in a hole can't find a spare battery.

Motor said nothing. But I watched his jaw.

"I can take almost anything," Bohdan said at last, very quietly. "The cold. The mud. The noise. I can take being scared. What I can't take is knowing that someone is making money from this."

"There are people who do," said Zenko. "There always have been. In every war."

"That doesn't make it acceptable."

"No. It never has."

I'm not naive about it. I know these things exist. Any large machine has people who skim it. But there's a particular sourness that comes from seeing it, even a rumor of it, from the bottom. A stolen winter jacket isn't a number. It's a man's hands, blue at the knuckles. A missing tourniquet isn't a line in a ledger. It's Halyna's face when she runs out.

And yet. I've also seen the opposite. Over and over, the opposite. A volunteer who spent his own money, down to his last coin, to buy a van. A quartermaster who stayed up all night to count blankets, so that nobody would be short. A young officer who gave away his own warm coat in January. Honest people, quietly doing right, in enormous numbers.

I think that's what keeps us from despair. The honest outnumber the thieves by a vast margin. We just hear less about them, because honesty doesn't make headlines. It makes socks.

On the night of the rumor, Vira said something that stuck. "I've decided," she said, "to be a person who'll be impossible to bribe. It's the only part of the war I control."

"That's very noble," said Bohdan.

"It's very *cheap,*" said Vira. "Nobody's offered me anything."

# Day 72 — Notes: Accountability While Fighting

A short, hard list.

**What we can't do:** Look away.

**What we shouldn't do:** Tear everything down in anger.

**What helps:**
1. **Write it down.** Dates, items, names.
2. **Tell someone who can act.** And follow up.
3. **Support the honest ones.** Loudly.
4. **Don't stop giving.** The thieves must not teach us to be stingy.
5. **Demand receipts.** From everyone, politely.
6. **Remember who's watching.** People who steal are afraid of being seen.

**What I believe:** The country we're fighting for has to be better than the one that started this. Otherwise, why?

How do you keep your anger useful?

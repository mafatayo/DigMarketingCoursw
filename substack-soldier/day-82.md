# Day 82 — Dispatch: Rebuilding Plans

Bohdan drew a bridge on the back of a ration box, and by dinner the entire dugout was designing the future.

It started so innocently. He was sketching, as he does, in the margins of everything, his sharp little pencil making clean quick lines. A bridge, a stone arch, graceful, with a footpath on one side. "I'd like to build that," he said, not really to anyone, "in the village. Where the old one fell."

Zenko leaned over. "It's very good."

"It's just a doodle."

"It's an arch. An arch is the oldest hope. Every arch says, *we shall cross this.*"

"Don't be poetic about my bridge."

"I'm being structurally poetic."

Within the hour, we'd all joined in. Vira wanted a school. A big bright building with proper windows and a library and a courtyard with one very old tree. She sketched it, badly, with obvious love. Halyna drew a clinic, low and white, with a veranda and a row of rocking chairs. Yaroslav, shy, drew a barn. Not a grand one. "A proper one," he said. "With a cow. Malvina's grand-daughter."

Motor didn't draw. But after a while, he took the pencil and, with enormous care, in his heavy workman's hand, drew a bus. A long, kind-faced, rounded bus, with a roof rack and a destination sign. In the window, a small face.

"What's the route?" I asked.

"To the end of the road," he said. "Where a woman waits with a thermos."

Nobody said anything. Zenko took off his glasses and polished them for a long time.

And me? I drew a small, simple room, with thick walls and a window that looked out on a garden. In the corner, a desk, with a pair of headphones hung on a peg, and a microphone on a stand, and a sign on the door: *QUIET. RECORDING.* It wasn't a grand plan. But it felt like my own.

We pinned the drawings to the wall, between the children's cards and the lists of names. A small, crooked gallery of the world that comes after. The Colonel sat in front of it and looked up, head tilted, for a long time, as if reading.

Maksym, the young lieutenant, wandered in later and stopped dead before the wall. He stood there, hands behind his back, for several minutes. Finally he said, quietly, "May I add one?"

He drew a small house, with a chimney and a garden gate. He wrote underneath, in tiny neat letters: *Mama's.*

# Day 82 — Notes: Engineers in Boots

Skills of the future, hiding in the present.

- **Bohdan:** Could rebuild a town's electronics from scrap.
- **Motor:** Could run a whole transport network.
- **Halyna:** Could staff a clinic by herself.
- **Vira:** Could organize a city.
- **Zenko:** Could teach a generation.
- **Yaroslav:** Could feed a village.
- **Me:** Could make it sound like peace.

**What I notice:** Everyone here is a builder. The army has simply put us temporarily in a different trade.

**What I hope:** That when the time comes, someone will hand us the shovels and the plans, and say, *go.*

What would you build?

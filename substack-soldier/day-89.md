*A work of fiction inspired by the accounts of Ukrainian soldiers.*

# Day 89 — Dispatch: Reader Mailbag

I've been saving up the questions, and tonight, with the lamp low and the stove ticking, I'll answer a few properly. Vira's reading them out loud in her best radio voice. Halyna insists we have tea first.

**"How do you keep going on the days you don't want to?"**

You don't keep going. You do the next thing. That's a different muscle. A kettle. A bucket. A letter. Nobody has to want to. They just have to start, and the wanting sometimes shows up later, like a late guest.

**"What's the funniest thing that's happened?"**

Hard to choose. The callsign. The wrench in the bread bin. The day Bohdan apologized to a hedgehog. But I think the winner is the night the whole radio net sang in dreadful harmony and the old officer in the north said "Is that a choir or an air raid?" and Vira, without pausing, said, "Both, sir. Both."

**"Is it true that soldiers don't talk about the hard things?"**

Partly. We talk sideways. We say "bad day." We pass each other tea. The hard things come out in odd corners, in a joke, a silence, a hand on a shoulder. You have to learn to hear them.

**"What would you say to someone who's thinking of joining?"**

Be honest with yourself about why. Learn first aid. Learn to sleep anywhere. Pack wool. Find a few good people. And call home.

**"What's the most important thing you've learned?"**

That the good people outnumber the others by a wide margin. I'd have bet otherwise on Day 1.

**"Do you ever think about quitting?"**

Constantly, in the sense that I think of every possible thing. I don't quit because of the faces. If I left, someone else would have to carry my bucket.

**"Does the dog know?"**

I believe he does. I believe he knows exactly what's going on and has decided to be calm about it, on all our behalf.

Halyna has asked me to add one more: *"Wash your hands."* She says she's received no questions about this and is concerned.

# Day 89 — Notes: Reader Mailbag, Part 2

A few more, quickly.

**"What's your favorite sound now?"** A kettle starting to hum.

**"What's your favorite smell?"** Fresh bread. A wet dog. Pickles.

**"What do you wish people would stop saying?"** "I can't imagine." Try. A little. Even badly.

**"What do you wish they'd say?"** "Tell me about the dog."

**"Anything you want to ask us?"** Yes. What are you doing this week to be kind to a stranger? I'd love to know. I'll read every answer by the lamp.
